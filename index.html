Day 15 스피킹

후회는 짧게 — should've / could've / would've

패턴 should have → should've (뜻: ~했어야 했는데) / could have → could've (뜻: ~할 수 있었는데) / would have → would've (뜻: ~했을 텐데) — 후회·아쉬움을 말할 때 쓰는 캐주얼 축약형

주의 발음은 shoulda/coulda/woulda처럼 편하게 하지만 글로 쓸 땐 should've — "should of"로 쓰지 않도록 주의. 이메일·에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 축약 안 씀.

예문 I should've called you. (전화했어야 했는데.) / I could've helped you. (도와줄 수 있었는데.) / She would've come, but she was busy. (걔 왔을 텐데, 바빴어.) / We shouldn't have left early. (일찍 가지 말았어야 했는데.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 should've / could've / would've 넣기

  1. I should have told you earlier. → I ___ told you earlier.
  2. She could have won the game. → She ___ won the game.
  3. We would have gone, but it rained. → We ___ gone, but it rained.
  4. He should have studied more. → He ___ studied more.
  5. They could have called first. → They ___ called first.
정답 보기

1. should've   2. could've   3. would've   4. should've   5. could've

질문 카드 — should've / could've / would've 써서 답해보기

  • What's something you should've done earlier this year?
  • Is there anything you could've done differently in your last job?
  • What's a trip you would've taken if you had more time or money?
  • What's something you should've said to someone but didn't?
  • Is there a decision you could've made better in the past?

Day 14 기본문법

~가 있다 — There is / There are

패턴 There is + 단수/셀 수 없는 명사 (뜻: ~가 있다) / There are + 복수명사 (뜻: ~들이 있다)

주의 의문문은 Is there ~? / Are there ~?, 부정문은 There isn't / There aren't ~ — 뒤에 오는 명사의 수에 동사를 맞춤.

예문 There is a cat outside. (밖에 고양이가 있어.) / There are two cups on the table. (테이블에 컵이 두 개 있어.) / Is there a bathroom near here? (근처에 화장실 있어요?) / There aren't any tickets left. (남은 티켓이 없어.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 There is / There are / Is there / Are there 넣기

  1. ___ a cat under the table.
  2. ___ five apples in the basket.
  3. ___ a good restaurant near here?
  4. ___ any seats left in the theater?
  5. ___ no milk in the fridge.
정답 보기

1. There is   2. There are   3. Is there   4. Are there   5. There isn't

질문 카드 — there is / there are 써서 답해보기

  • Is there a good coffee shop near your house?
  • Are there any pets at your home?
  • Is there anything you want to buy this week?
  • Are there many restaurants in your neighborhood?
  • Is there a place you really want to visit someday?

Day 13 어미·표현

계속 해오고 있다 — 현재완료진행형 have been -ing

패턴 have/has been + V-ing + for/since (뜻: (계속) ~해오고 있다) — 과거에 시작해서 지금까지 계속되는 일을 말할 때 씀

주의 for는 기간(3 years, an hour)과 함께, since는 시작 시점(2020, yesterday) 뒤에 옴. know/like/believe 같은 상태동사는 진행형 불가 — 그냥 have p.p.로 씀.

예문 I have been studying English for 3 years. (나는 3년째 영어를 공부해오고 있어.) / She has been living here since 2020. (그녀는 2020년부터 여기서 살고 있어.) / We have been waiting for an hour. (우리는 한 시간째 기다리고 있어.) / He has been working since morning. (그는 아침부터 일하고 있어.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 have/has been -ing 형태로 넣기

  1. I ___ (study) English for 3 years.
  2. She ___ (live) here since 2020.
  3. We ___ (wait) for an hour.
  4. He ___ (work) since 7 am.
  5. They ___ (play) soccer for two hours.
정답 보기

1. have been studying   2. has been living   3. have been waiting   4. has been working   5. have been playing

질문 카드 — have been -ing 써서 답해보기

  • How long have you been studying English?
  • How long have you been living in your current place?
  • What's something you've been doing a lot lately?
  • How long have you been working at your job?
  • What have you been doing since this morning?

Day 12 스피킹

편하게 부정하기 — ain't

패턴 am not/is not/are not → ain't (뜻: ~아니야) / has not/have not → ain't (뜻: ~하지 않았어) — 부정문을 한 단어로 퉁치는 캐주얼 표현

주의 매우 캐주얼하고 비격식적인 표현 — 문법적으로 "틀린" 형태로 여겨지므로 이메일·에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 절대 안 씀.

예문 I ain't going. (안 갈 거야.) / She ain't home. (걔 집에 없어.) / We ain't done yet. (아직 안 끝났어.) / That ain't right. (그건 아니지.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 ain't 넣기

  1. I am not going to the party. → I ___ going to the party.
  2. She is not home right now. → She ___ home right now.
  3. We are not ready yet. → We ___ ready yet.
  4. He has not finished his homework. → He ___ finished his homework.
  5. They have not called me back. → They ___ called me back.
정답 보기

1. ain't   2. ain't   3. ain't   4. ain't   5. ain't

질문 카드 — ain't 써서 답해보기

  • What's something you ain't done yet but really wanna try?
  • Is there any food you ain't tried before?
  • What's a place you ain't been to yet but wanna visit?
  • Is there anything you ain't good at but wish you were?
  • What's something you ain't got time for these days?

Day 11 어미·표현

더 크다, 더 어렵다 — 비교급 -er vs more

패턴 1음절 형용사 + -er (뜻: 더 ~한) / 2음절 이상 형용사는 more + 형용사 (뜻: 더 ~한)

주의 불규칙 비교급 조심 — good→better, bad→worse, far→farther. -er와 more를 같이 쓰지 않음 (more bigger는 틀린 표현).

예문 This bag is bigger than that one. (이 가방이 더 커요.) / This test is more difficult than the last one. (이 시험이 더 어려워요.) / She is taller than me. (그녀가 나보다 커요.) / This coffee is better than that one. (이 커피가 더 맛있어요.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 비교급 넣기

  1. This bag is ___ (big) than that one.
  2. My phone is ___ (expensive) than yours.
  3. She is ___ (tall) than her brother.
  4. This movie was ___ (interesting) than the last one.
  5. Today is ___ (cold) than yesterday.
정답 보기

1. bigger   2. more expensive   3. taller   4. more interesting   5. colder

질문 카드 — 비교급 써서 답해보기

  • Which is bigger, your hometown or Seoul?
  • Are you taller than your mom or your dad?
  • What's more expensive for you, coffee or lunch?
  • Is Korean grammar more difficult than English grammar for you?
  • What's better on a rainy day, staying home or going out?

Day 10 기본문법

Wh-의문사로 질문 만들기 — who/what/where/when/why/how

패턴 who(누가·누구를) / what(무엇을) / where(어디서) / when(언제) / why(왜) / how(어떻게) + do/does/did + 주어 + 동사원형?

주의 의문사 뒤에도 do/does/did 순서는 그대로 지킴 — 동사는 항상 원형으로.

예문 Where do you live? (어디 사세요?) / What do you do? (무슨 일 하세요?) / Why did you choose this job? (왜 이 직업을 선택했어요?) / How do you get to work? (어떻게 출근하세요?)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 알맞은 의문사 넣기

  1. ___ do you live?
  2. ___ do you do for work?
  3. ___ is your birthday?
  4. ___ did you choose this job?
  5. ___ do you get to work?
정답 보기

1. Where   2. What   3. When   4. Why   5. How

질문 카드 — 의문사 질문에 답해보기

  • Where do you live now? How did you come here today?
  • What do you do? Do you like your job?
  • What's your favorite day of the week, and why?
  • What month do you like the most? Why do you like it?
  • How often do you go on trips? Where do you like to travel?

Day 9 스피킹

부탁할 때 쓰는 축약형 — gimme / lemme

패턴 give me → gimme (뜻: ~ 줘) / let me → lemme (뜻: ~할게, ~하게 해줘) — 부탁하거나 허락 구할 때 쓰는 캐주얼한 축약형

주의 편하게 말할 때만 씀. 이메일·에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 give me / let me 그대로 씀.

예문 Gimme a second. (잠깐만.) / Lemme think about it. (생각 좀 해볼게.) / Gimme your phone number. (번호 좀 줘.) / Lemme know. (알려줘.)

연습 10개 — give me / let me를 캐주얼 축약형으로 바꿔 말해보기

  1. Give me a second. → ___ a second.
  2. Let me think about it. → ___ think about it.
  3. Give me your phone number. → ___ your phone number.
  4. Let me help you with that. → ___ help you with that.
  5. Give me a break. → ___ a break.
  6. Let me see that. → ___ see that.
  7. Give me a hand with this box. → ___ a hand with this box.
  8. Let me know when you're ready. → ___ know when you're ready.
  9. Give me one more chance. → ___ one more chance.
  10. Let me finish my coffee first. → ___ finish my coffee first.
정답 보기

1. Gimme   2. Lemme   3. Gimme   4. Lemme   5. Gimme   6. Lemme   7. Gimme   8. Lemme   9. Gimme   10. Lemme

Day 8 어미·표현

예전엔 이랬어 — used to vs would

패턴 used to + 동사원형 (과거의 습관·상태), would + 동사원형 (과거의 반복된 행동)

주의 would는 live, like, have, know 같은 상태동사에는 못 씀 — 상태를 말할 땐 무조건 used to

예문 I used to live in Seoul. / I used to be shy. / Every summer, I would visit my grandma.

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 used to 또는 would 넣기

  1. I ___ live in a small town. (상태 — used to만 가능)
  2. Every Friday, my mom ___ make pancakes for us. (반복된 행동)
  3. She ___ be afraid of dogs, but not anymore. (상태 — used to만 가능)
  4. When I was a kid, I ___ visit my grandparents every summer. (반복된 행동)
  5. He ___ have a beard, but he shaved it off. (상태 — used to만 가능)
정답 보기

1. used to   2. would / used to   3. used to   4. would / used to   5. used to

질문 카드 — used to 써서 답해보기

  • What's something you used to do as a kid but don't do anymore?
  • Did you use to have a favorite toy when you were little?
  • What food did you use to hate but like now?
  • Where did you use to live before you moved here?
  • Did you use to be into any different hobbies in the past?

Day 6 스피킹

애매하게 말하기 — kinda / sorta

패턴 kind of → kinda / sort of → sorta — 정도를 애매하게, 부드럽게 말할 때 쓰는 캐주얼 표현

주의 말할 때만 쓰는 표현. 이메일이나 에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 kind of / sort of 그대로 씀.

예문 I'm kinda tired. / It's sorta cold today. / I kinda like it.

  3. She is kind of upset today. → She's ___ upset today.
  4. It's sort of expensive. → It's ___ expensive.
  5. I kind of want to go home. → I ___ want to go home.

  • Are you kinda tired today?
  • Do you kinda like spicy food?
  • Is your job sorta stressful these days?
  • Are you kinda nervous about anything right now?

Day 5 어미·표현

지루한 거야, 지루하게 하는 거야? — -ing vs -ed 감정 형용사

패턴 감정을 느끼는 사람 → -ed (bored) / 감정을 일으키는 원인 → -ing (boring)

예문 I'm bored. (내가 지루함을 느낀다) / This movie is boring. (이 영화가 지루하게 만든다)

연습 10개 — 빈칸에 -ing 또는 -ed 형태로 넣기

  1. This class is so ___. (bore)
  2. I'm really ___ right now. (bore)
  3. The news was ___. (shock)
  4. She was ___ to hear the results. (shock)
  5. This book is ___. (interest)
  6. I'm ___ in learning Spanish. (interest)
  7. The movie was ___. (excite)
  8. The kids were ___ about the trip. (excite)
  9. His story is ___. (amaze)
  10. I was ___ by his story. (amaze)

  • What's something you're really interested in these days?
  • What was the most exciting day of your life so far?
  • What's the most amazing place you've ever visited?

Day 2 어미·표현

얼마나 자주 하는지 말하기 — 빈도부사

패턴 빈도부사는 일반동사 앞, be동사 뒤에 온다. (I usually go / I am usually tired)

빈도 스케일 always(100%) – usually(80%) – often(60%) – sometimes(40%) – not often(20%) – rarely(10%) – hardly ever(5%) – never(0%)

"once a week" 다르게 말하기 횟수 + a + 기간 → once a day/week/month/year, twice a week/month/year, three times a week/month/year

연습 10개 — 질문에 빈도 표현 넣어서 답해보기

  • Do you play any sports? How often do you play?
  • How often do you go on trips? Where do you like to travel?
  • How often do you eat out? What's your favorite restaurant?
  • Do you go to the movies often? What kind of movies do you like best?
  • What do you usually do when you have free time?
  • What do you usually do on Saturdays?
  • What do you usually do on your birthday?
  • What's your favorite day of the week, and why?
  • What do you usually do in the evening?
  • What time do you usually go to bed?

연습 10개 — 빈칸에 넣어 말해보기

  1. 나는 커피를 좋아해. → I ___ coffee.
  2. 그는 그녀를 믿어. → He ___ her.
  3. 우리는 그 영화를 봤어. → We ___ the movie.
  4. 그들은 답을 알아. → They ___ the answer.
  5. 나는 널 이해해. → I ___ you.
  6. 그녀는 개를 키워. → She ___ a dog.
  7. 우리는 그 계획을 좋아해. → We ___ the plan.
  8. 그는 진실을 말했어. → He ___ the truth.
  9. 나는 그 노래를 알아. → I ___ that song.
  10. 그들은 그 집을 샀어. → They ___ the house.
