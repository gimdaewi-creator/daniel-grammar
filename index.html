Day 58 기본문법

즐기는 건 -ing, 원하는 건 to — 동명사 vs to부정사

패턴 enjoy (뜻: ~하는 걸 즐기다) / finish (뜻: ~하는 걸 끝내다) / stop (뜻: ~하는 걸 그만두다) / avoid (뜻: ~하는 걸 피하다) + 동사-ing  |  want (뜻: ~하고 싶다) / decide (뜻: ~하기로 하다) / hope (뜻: ~하길 바라다) / plan (뜻: ~할 계획이다) + to 동사

주의 동사마다 뒤에 오는 모양이 정해져 있어요. like·love·start는 둘 다 OK: I like cooking. = I like to cook.

예문 I enjoy listening to music. (음악 듣는 걸 즐겨.) / I want to travel. (여행 가고 싶어.) / It stopped raining. (비가 그쳤어.) / We decided to eat out. (외식하기로 했어.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. I enjoy ___ movies. (watch)
  2. I want ___ home early. (go)
  3. She finished ___ her homework. (do)
  4. We decided ___ out tonight. (eat)
  5. I hope ___ you soon. (see)
정답 보기

1. watching   2. to go   3. doing   4. to eat   5. to see

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What do you enjoy doing on weekends?
  • Where do you want to travel next?
  • What did you decide to do this year?
  • What do you hope to do in the future?
  • Is there something you want to stop doing? Why?

Day 57 어미·표현

동사 + 작은 단어 한 덩어리 — 구동사 look for / pick up / give up

패턴 look for (뜻: ~을 찾다) / pick up (뜻: ~을 집다, ~를 데리러 가다) / turn on·off (뜻: ~을 켜다·끄다) / give up (뜻: 포기하다) / find out (뜻: 알아내다)

주의 동사 + 작은 단어가 한 덩어리 뜻이에요. 대명사는 가운데에 와요: turn it off (O) / turn off it (X).

예문 I'm looking for my keys. (열쇠 찾는 중이야.) / Can you pick me up at six? (6시에 데리러 와 줄래?) / Turn it off, please. (그것 좀 꺼 줘.) / Don't give up! (포기하지 마!)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. I can't find my phone. I'm looking ___ it. (for / at)
  2. Can you pick me ___ at six? (up / on)
  3. It's too loud. Turn it ___, please. (off / of)
  4. Don't give ___! You can do it. (up / out)
  5. I want to find ___ the answer. (out / in)
정답 보기

1. for   2. up   3. off   4. up   5. out

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What are you looking for these days?
  • Who picks you up after class or work?
  • What do you turn off before you go to bed?
  • Is there something you gave up? Why did you give it up?
  • How do you find out about new restaurants?

Day 56 스피킹

좋아, 나도 갈래 — I'm down / I'm in / Count me in

패턴 I'm down (뜻: 나 좋아, 콜) / I'm in (뜻: 나도 낄래) / Count me in (뜻: 나도 끼워 줘) / Works for me (뜻: 난 괜찮아) / I'm not up for it (뜻: 별로 안 당겨)

주의 친구끼리 캐주얼하게 쓰는 말이에요. 격식 있는 자리에선 "That would be great."처럼 말해요.

예문 Pizza tonight? I'm down. (오늘 피자? 콜.) / We're going hiking. I'm in! (우리 등산 가. 나도 갈래!) / Count me in! (나도 끼워 줘!) / Saturday works for me. (토요일 난 괜찮아.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. Movie tonight? I'm ___. (down / up)
  2. We're playing soccer. Count ___ in! (me / my)
  3. Sunday ___ for me. (works / work)
  4. Let's get dinner. I'm ___! (in / on)
  5. I'm tired. I'm not ___ for it. (up / in)
정답 보기

1. down   2. me   3. works   4. in   5. up

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • Your friend says, "Let's get coffee after class." How do you say yes?
  • Someone invites you to a Saturday party. Does Saturday work for you?
  • What are you always down for on a free evening?
  • Your friends plan a weekend trip. How do you say you want to join?
  • What is something you are not up for today?

Day 55 기본문법

그는 해, 그녀는 해 — 3인칭 단수 동사 -s / -es

패턴 I/you/we/they + 동사 (뜻: ~해) / he·she·it + 동사-s (뜻: 그/그녀/그것은 ~해) / watch → watches, go → goes (뜻: 보다, 가다) / study → studies (뜻: 공부하다) / have → has (뜻: 가지다)

주의 does/doesn't 뒤에서는 -s가 does로 넘어가서 동사는 원형이에요 (He doesn't play.).

예문 She works at a bank. (그녀는 은행에서 일해.) / He watches TV every night. (그는 매일 밤 TV를 봐.) / My brother studies English. (우리 형은 영어를 공부해.) / My dog has a blue ball. (우리 개는 파란 공이 있어.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. My sister ___ coffee every morning. (drink)
  2. He ___ TV after dinner. (watch)
  3. She ___ English at school. (study)
  4. My dad ___ to work by bus. (go)
  5. The cat ___ a small bed. (have)
정답 보기

1. drinks   2. watches   3. studies   4. goes   5. has

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What does your best friend do on Saturdays? Say "He/She ...".
  • What time does your mom or dad usually go to bed?
  • Does your brother or sister play any sports? What does he/she play?
  • What does your favorite singer do in free time? Start with "He/She ...".
  • What does your pet do all day? Use "He/She/It ...".

Day 54 어미·표현

정말 ~한! — so vs such

패턴 so + 형용사/부사 (뜻: 정말 ~한) / such + a/an + 형용사 + 명사 (뜻: 정말 ~한 …) / so ... that ~ (뜻: 너무 ~해서 ~해)

주의 뒤에 명사가 있으면 such, 형용사만 있으면 so를 써요. 복수나 셀 수 없는 명사는 a/an 없이 such만 붙여요.

예문 It's so hot today. (오늘 정말 더워.) / She is such a nice person. (그녀는 정말 좋은 사람이야.) / This is such good pizza! (이거 정말 맛있는 피자야!) / I was so tired that I fell asleep. (너무 피곤해서 잠들었어.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. It's ___ hot today. (so / such)
  2. She is ___ a nice person. (so / such)
  3. This is ___ good pizza! (so / such)
  4. I was ___ tired that I fell asleep. (so / such)
  5. They are ___ friendly people. (so / such)
정답 보기

1. so   2. such   3. such   4. so   5. such

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What food is so delicious that you eat it every week? Say "It's so ...".
  • Who is such a funny person in your life? Say "He/She is such a ...".
  • What was such a great day for you? Tell your partner about it.
  • What is so expensive that you can't buy it? Use "It's so ...".
  • How is the weather today? Start with "It's so ...".

Day 53 스피킹

듣기엔 좋은데, 보기엔 피곤해 — sounds / looks / feels

패턴 sounds / looks / feels / smells / tastes + 형용사 (뜻: ~하게 들려/보여/느껴져/냄새나/맛나) / sounds / looks like + 명사 (뜻: ~ 같아)

주의 감각동사 뒤에는 부사가 아니라 형용사가 와요. 명사가 오면 꼭 like를 붙여요. 캐주얼한 대화에선 That을 빼고 "Sounds good!"처럼 짧게 말해요.

예문 Sounds good! (좋아!) / You look tired. (너 피곤해 보여.) / It looks like rain. (비 올 것 같아.) / That sounds like a good plan. (좋은 계획 같아.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸 채우기

  1. That ___ fun. (sounds / sound)
  2. You ___ tired today. (look / looks)
  3. It looks ___ rain. (like / as)
  4. This soup ___ good. (smells / smell)
  5. That sounds ___ a great idea. (like / for)
정답 보기

1. sounds   2. look   3. like   4. smells   5. like

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • Your friend says, "Let's go to the movies tonight." How do you answer with "Sounds ..."?
  • What does your favorite food smell like? Use "It smells ...".
  • How do you feel today? Say "I feel ...".
  • Look at your partner. How do they look today? Say "You look ...".
  • What does the weather look like today? Use "It looks like ...".

Day 52 기본문법

명령하고, 권유하고, 말려 — 명령문 / Let's / Don't

패턴 동사원형 (뜻: ~해) / Don't + 동사원형 (뜻: ~하지 마) / Let's + 동사원형 (뜻: 우리 ~하자) / Let's not + 동사원형 (뜻: ~하지 말자)

주의 명령문은 주어(You) 없이 동사로 시작해요. 문장 앞이나 끝에 please를 붙이면 훨씬 공손해져요.

예문 Be careful. (조심해.) / Don't be late. (늦지 마.) / Let's go to the park. (공원에 가자.) / Let's not argue. (말다툼하지 말자.)

연습 5개 — 알맞은 말 고르기

  1. (Be / Are) careful with that cup.
  2. (Don't / Not) forget your umbrella.
  3. (Let's / Let) go to the park.
  4. Let's (not / don't) argue about this.
  5. Please (sit / sits) down.
정답 보기

1. Be   2. Don't   3. Let's   4. not   5. sit

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What should we do together this weekend? Start with "Let's".
  • Where should we eat tonight? Suggest a place with "Let's".
  • What is one thing your friend should never forget? Say "Don't forget to ...".
  • What is a bad habit people should stop? Say "Don't ...".
  • Give your partner three simple commands, like "Stand up" or "Open the window".

Day 51 어미·표현

시키고, 허락하고, 부탁해 — make / let / have + 목적어 + 동사원형

패턴 make + 사람 + 동사원형 (뜻: ~가 …하게 만들다, 억지로 시키다) / let + 사람 + 동사원형 (뜻: ~가 …하게 허락하다) / have + 사람 + 동사원형 (뜻: ~에게 …해 달라고 시키다, 부탁하다)

주의 세 동사 모두 목적어 뒤에 to 없이 동사원형이 와요. make는 강제, let은 허락, have는 일상적인 부탁 느낌이에요.

예문 My mom makes me clean my room. (엄마는 내가 방을 청소하게 만들어.) / Let me help you. (내가 도와줄게.) / I had him fix my phone. (그에게 내 폰을 고쳐 달라고 했어.) / The movie made me cry. (그 영화 때문에 울었어.)

연습 5개 — 알맞은 말 고르기

  1. My boss makes me (work / to work) on Saturdays.
  2. Please let me (go / to go) home early.
  3. I had my friend (help / to help) me move.
  4. That song always makes me (smile / smiling).
  5. My parents don't let me (stay / to stay) out late.
정답 보기

1. work   2. go   3. help   4. smile   5. stay

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • What kind of music makes you happy?
  • What do your parents let you do on your birthday?
  • What makes you tired after work?
  • Do you let your friends borrow your things? What do you never let them borrow?
  • What do you usually have someone do for you, like cooking or fixing things?

Day 50 스피킹

말 돌리고 이어가기 — Anyway / By the way / Speaking of / Actually

패턴 Anyway (뜻: 어쨌든, 아무튼) / By the way (뜻: 그건 그렇고) / Speaking of ~ (뜻: ~ 얘기가 나왔으니 말인데) / Actually (뜻: 사실은)

주의 대화의 흐름을 바꾸거나 이어 줄 때 문장 맨 앞에 붙이는 말이에요. 친구와의 대화에서 아주 자주 쓰여요.

예문 Anyway, I have to go. (어쨌든 나 가야 해.) / By the way, I saw your sister. (그건 그렇고, 네 여동생 봤어.) / Speaking of food, I'm hungry. (음식 얘기 나왔으니 말인데, 배고파.) / Actually, I don't like it. (사실은 그거 안 좋아해.)

연습 5개 — 알맞은 표현 고르기

  1. (Anyway / Actually), I have to go now. See you!
  2. Oh, (by the way / speaking of), I saw your sister yesterday.
  3. You like movies? (Speaking of / Anyway) movies, have you seen the new one?
  4. I thought it was easy, but (actually / anyway) it was really hard.
  5. That's enough about work. (Anyway / Actually), how was your weekend?
정답 보기

1. Anyway   2. by the way   3. Speaking of   4. actually   5. Anyway

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • Anyway, what do you usually do on Saturdays?
  • By the way, what's your favorite restaurant? How often do you eat out?
  • Speaking of food, what's your favorite food? Can you cook it?
  • Actually, do you like your job? Tell me the real answer.
  • Speaking of movies, do you go to the movies often? What kind do you like best?

Day 49 기본문법

나는 vs 나를 — 주격 vs 목적격 대명사 (I / me)

패턴 동사 앞(주어): I (나는) / you (너는) / he (그는) / she (그녀는) / we (우리는) / they (그들은) · 동사·전치사 뒤: me (나를) / you (너를) / him (그를) / her (그녀를) / us (우리를) / them (그들을)

주의 and로 이어질 때도 헷갈려요. "Me and him go"가 아니라 "He and I go", 그리고 "with he"가 아니라 "with him"이에요.

예문 She loves me. (그녀는 나를 사랑해.) / I called him yesterday. (어제 그에게 전화했어.) / Come with us. (우리랑 같이 가.) / They don't know her. (그들은 그녀를 몰라.)

연습 5개 — 알맞은 대명사 고르기

  1. (I / me) love pizza.
  2. Call (I / me) tonight.
  3. Do you know (he / him)?
  4. (She / Her) works with (we / us).
  5. Can you help (they / them)?
정답 보기

1. I   2. me   3. him   4. She / us   5. them

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • Do you have any siblings? Do you call them often?
  • Who do you usually eat dinner with? What do you talk about with them?
  • Do you have a friend you see every week? What do you do with him or her?
  • What do you usually do with your family on your birthday? Do they give you a gift?
  • Who do you text the most? How often do you text him or her?

Day 48 어미·표현

누구, 무엇을 설명해 — 관계대명사 who / which / that

패턴 명사 + who + 동사 (뜻: ~하는 사람) · 명사 + which / that + 동사 (뜻: ~하는 것) · 사람은 who, 물건은 which, 둘 다 that 가능

주의 말할 때는 사람에게 who, 물건에게 that을 가장 많이 써요. which는 조금 더 격식 있게 들려요.

예문 I have a friend who lives in Busan. (부산에 사는 친구가 있어.) / This is the book that I told you about. (이게 내가 말했던 책이야.) / She is the teacher who teaches English. (그분이 영어를 가르치는 선생님이야.) / I like movies that make me laugh. (나는 웃게 만드는 영화가 좋아.)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 who / which / that 넣기

  1. She is the teacher ___ teaches English.
  2. This is the book ___ I told you about.
  3. I have a friend ___ lives in Busan.
  4. That's the phone ___ was on sale.
  5. He's the guy ___ sings in our band.
정답 보기

1. who (또는 that)   2. that (또는 which)   3. who (또는 that)   4. that (또는 which)   5. who (또는 that)

질문 카드 — 짝과 말해 보기

  • Do you have a friend who lives abroad? What is he or she like?
  • What's a restaurant that you like? How often do you eat out?
  • Do you have a favorite singer who you listen to a lot? What kind of music do you like?
  • What's a movie that you want to see? Do you go to the movies often?
  • Do you know someone who plays a sport? How often do they play?

Day 47 스피킹

잠깐만, 어서, 그래 해 — Hang on / Come on / Go ahead

패턴 Hang on / Hold on (뜻: 잠깐만 기다려) · Come on (뜻: 어서, 제발, 힘내) · Go ahead (뜻: 그래, 먼저 해) · Hurry up (뜻: 서둘러) · Check it out (뜻: 이것 좀 봐)

주의 모두 친구·가족끼리 쓰는 편한 말이에요. 윗사람에게는 "Just a moment, please."처럼 공손하게 말해요.

예문 Hang on a second. (잠깐만 기다려.) / Come on, let's go! (어서, 가자!) / Can I ask a question? Go ahead. (질문해도 돼? 그래, 해.) / Check it out! (이것 좀 봐!)

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 알맞은 말 넣기

  1. A: Can I use your pen? B: Sure, ___ ahead.
  2. ___ on! You're going to be late for class.
  3. A: Are you ready? B: ___ on, I need to find my keys.
  4. ___ up! The movie starts in five minutes.

  2. My phone is ___ (expensive) than yours.
  3. She is ___ (tall) than her brother.
  4. This movie was ___ (interesting) than the last one.
  5. Today is ___ (cold) than yesterday.

1. bigger   2. more expensive   3. taller   4. more interesting   5. colder

  • Are you taller than your mom or your dad?
  • What's more expensive for you, coffee or lunch?
  • Is Korean grammar more difficult than English grammar for you?
  • What's better on a rainy day, staying home or going out?
