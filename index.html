Day 9 스피킹

부탁할 때 쓰는 축약형 — gimme / lemme

패턴 give me [기브 미] → gimme [기미] / let me [렛 미] → lemme [레미] — 뭔가를 달라고 하거나("~줘") 허락을 구할 때("~하게 해줘") 캐주얼하게 줄여 말하는 표현

뜻 gimme = give me = "(나한테) ~ 줘" — 상대방이 나에게 뭔가를 주길 바랄 때. / lemme = let me = "내가 ~하게 해줘, 나 ~할게" — 내가 뭔가를 하도록 허락을 구하거나 자연스럽게 제안할 때.

왜 줄어드나요 give me, let me를 빠르게 이어 말하면 두 단어의 소리가 뭉개지면서 하나의 단어처럼 들림. 그 소리를 그대로 철자로 옮긴 게 gimme, lemme — 실제 원어민 대화, 노래 가사, 문자에서 아주 흔하게 보임.

구조 차이 gimme 뒤에는 명사(목적어)가 바로 옴 → Gimme a second. (초 하나를 달라는 게 아니라 "잠깐만"이라는 뜻) / lemme 뒤에는 동사원형이 옴 → Lemme think. ("내가 생각 좀 해볼게") — 이 순서를 바꾸면 어색함.

주의 아주 캐주얼한 구어체 표현. 친한 친구·가족 사이에서만 쓰고, 이메일·에세이 같은 격식 있는 글이나 손윗사람·처음 만난 사이에는 give me / let me 그대로 씀.

예문 Gimme a second. (잠깐만 시간 줘.) / Lemme think about it. (내가 한번 생각해볼게.) / Gimme your phone number. (전화번호 좀 줘.) / Lemme know if you need help. (도움 필요하면 알려줘.)

연습 10개 — give me / let me를 캐주얼 축약형으로 바꿔 말해보기

  1. Give me a second. → ___ a second. (잠깐만 시간 줘.)
  2. Let me think about it. → ___ think about it. (내가 생각 좀 해볼게.)
  3. Give me your phone number. → ___ your phone number. (전화번호 좀 줘.)
  4. Let me help you with that. → ___ help you with that. (내가 그거 도와줄게.)
  5. Give me a break. → ___ a break. (좀 봐줘 / 그만 좀 해.)
  6. Let me see that. → ___ see that. (그거 나 좀 보여줘.)
  7. Give me a hand with this box. → ___ a hand with this box. (이 박스 옮기는 것 좀 도와줘.)
  8. Let me know when you're ready. → ___ know when you're ready. (준비되면 나한테 알려줘.)
  9. Give me one more chance. → ___ one more chance. (한 번만 더 기회를 줘.)
  10. Let me finish my coffee first. → ___ finish my coffee first. (커피부터 다 마시고.)

Day 8 어미·표현

예전엔 이랬어 — used to vs would

패턴 used to + 동사원형 (과거의 습관·상태), would + 동사원형 (과거의 반복된 행동)

주의 would는 live, like, have, know 같은 상태동사에는 못 씀 — 상태를 말할 땐 무조건 used to

예문 I used to live in Seoul. / I used to be shy. / Every summer, I would visit my grandma.

연습 5개 — 빈칸에 used to 또는 would 넣기

  1. I ___ live in a small town. (상태 — used to만 가능)
  2. Every Friday, my mom ___ make pancakes for us. (반복된 행동)
  3. She ___ be afraid of dogs, but not anymore. (상태 — used to만 가능)
  4. When I was a kid, I ___ visit my grandparents every summer. (반복된 행동)
  5. He ___ have a beard, but he shaved it off. (상태 — used to만 가능)
정답 보기

1. used to   2. would / used to   3. used to   4. would / used to   5. used to

질문 카드 — used to 써서 답해보기

  • What's something you used to do as a kid but don't do anymore?
  • Did you use to have a favorite toy when you were little?
  • What food did you use to hate but like now?
  • Where did you use to live before you moved here?
  • Did you use to be into any different hobbies in the past?

Day 7 기본문법

시간 전치사 — in / on / at

패턴 in (월/계절/연도/아침·오후·저녁), on (요일/날짜), at (구체적 시각)

주의 at night은 "저녁에"인데도 at을 씀 — 예외로 그냥 외우기

예문 in July / on Monday / at 7 o'clock

연습 10개 — 빈칸에 in / on / at 넣기

  1. I was born ___ 1998.
  2. My birthday is ___ July.
  3. Let's meet ___ Monday.
  4. She wakes up ___ 7 o'clock.
  5. We go on vacation ___ summer.
  6. The meeting is ___ March 3rd.
  7. I usually study ___ the morning.
  8. He works late ___ night.
  9. See you ___ Friday!
  10. The store opens ___ 10 am.

Day 6 스피킹

애매하게 말하기 — kinda / sorta

패턴 kind of → kinda / sort of → sorta — 정도를 애매하게, 부드럽게 말할 때 쓰는 캐주얼 표현

주의 말할 때만 쓰는 표현. 이메일이나 에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 kind of / sort of 그대로 씀.

예문 I'm kinda tired. / It's sorta cold today. / I kinda like it.

연습 5개 — kind of / sort of를 캐주얼 축약형으로 바꿔 말해보기

  1. I am kind of hungry. → I'm ___ hungry.
  2. This movie is sort of boring. → This movie is ___ boring.
  3. She is kind of upset today. → She's ___ upset today.
  4. It's sort of expensive. → It's ___ expensive.
  5. I kind of want to go home. → I ___ want to go home.
정답 보기

1. kinda   2. sorta   3. kinda   4. sorta   5. kinda

질문 카드 — kinda / sorta 써서 답해보기

  • Are you kinda tired today?
  • Do you kinda like spicy food?
  • Is your job sorta stressful these days?
  • Are you kinda nervous about anything right now?
  • Do you sorta wanna travel somewhere new this year?

Day 5 어미·표현

지루한 거야, 지루하게 하는 거야? — -ing vs -ed 감정 형용사

패턴 감정을 느끼는 사람 → -ed (bored) / 감정을 일으키는 원인 → -ing (boring)

예문 I'm bored. (내가 지루함을 느낀다) / This movie is boring. (이 영화가 지루하게 만든다)

연습 10개 — 빈칸에 -ing 또는 -ed 형태로 넣기

  1. This class is so ___. (bore)
  2. I'm really ___ right now. (bore)
  3. The news was ___. (shock)
  4. She was ___ to hear the results. (shock)
  5. This book is ___. (interest)
  6. I'm ___ in learning Spanish. (interest)
  7. The movie was ___. (excite)
  8. The kids were ___ about the trip. (excite)
  9. His story is ___. (amaze)
  10. I was ___ by his story. (amaze)
정답 보기

1. boring   2. bored   3. shocking   4. shocked   5. interesting   6. interested   7. exciting   8. excited   9. amazing   10. amazed

질문 카드 — -ing / -ed 감정 형용사 써서 답해보기

  • What's the most boring class you've ever taken?
  • Have you ever been really shocked by something? What happened?
  • What's something you're really interested in these days?
  • What was the most exciting day of your life so far?
  • What's the most amazing place you've ever visited?
  • Do you get bored easily? What do you do about it?

Day 4 스피킹

말할 때 진짜 쓰는 축약형 — gonna / wanna / gotta

패턴 going to → gonna / want to → wanna / got to (have to) → gotta

주의 편하게 말할 때만 쓰는 캐주얼한 표현. 이메일이나 에세이 같은 격식 있는 글에는 안 씀.

예문 I'm gonna go home. / I wanna eat pizza. / I gotta go now.

연습 10개 — 격식체를 캐주얼 축약형으로 바꿔 말해보기

  1. I am going to watch a movie tonight. → I'm ___ watch a movie tonight.
  2. Do you want to come with us? → Do you ___ come with us?
  3. I have got to finish this today. → I ___ finish this today.
  4. She is going to call you later. → She's ___ call you later.
  5. I want to try that new restaurant. → I ___ try that new restaurant.
  6. We have got to leave now. → We ___ leave now.
  7. He is going to be late. → He's ___ be late.
  8. I want to sleep in tomorrow. → I ___ sleep in tomorrow.
  9. They are going to move next month. → They're ___ move next month.
  10. You have got to see this. → You ___ see this.
정답 보기

1. gonna   2. wanna   3. gotta   4. gonna   5. wanna   6. gotta   7. gonna   8. wanna   9. gonna   10. gotta

질문 카드 — gonna / wanna 써서 답해보기

  • What are you gonna do this weekend?
  • What do you wanna eat for dinner tonight?
  • Are you gonna watch anything good this week?
  • What are you gonna do after this class?
  • Do you wanna hang out with friends this weekend?
  • What are you gonna do when you get home today?

Day 3 기본문법

Do / Does로 질문하기

패턴 Do + I/you/we/they + 동사원형 ~? / Does + he/she/it + 동사원형 ~?

핵심 does를 쓰면 동사는 항상 원형 (좋아한다 = likes 아니고 like)

1~5. 카드 질문 그대로 답해보기

  • Do you have any siblings? What are they like?
  • Do you have any pets? Can you talk about them?
  • Do you play any sports? What's your favorite one?
  • Do you go to the movies often?
  • Do you like your job?

6~10. 문장을 Do/Does 의문문으로 바꾸기

  1. She likes pizza. → ___ she like pizza?
  2. He plays soccer. → ___ he play soccer?
  3. They have a dog. → ___ they have a dog?
  4. He goes to the gym. → ___ he go to the gym?
  5. She works on Saturdays. → ___ she work on Saturdays?
정답 보기 (6~10)

6. Does   7. Does   8. Do   9. Does   10. Does

Day 2 어미·표현

얼마나 자주 하는지 말하기 — 빈도부사

패턴 빈도부사는 일반동사 앞, be동사 뒤에 온다. (I usually go / I am usually tired)

빈도 스케일 always(100%) – usually(80%) – often(60%) – sometimes(40%) – not often(20%) – rarely(10%) – hardly ever(5%) – never(0%)

"once a week" 다르게 말하기 횟수 + a + 기간 → once a day/week/month/year, twice a week/month/year, three times a week/month/year

연습 10개 — 질문에 빈도 표현 넣어서 답해보기

  • Do you play any sports? How often do you play?
  • How often do you go on trips? Where do you like to travel?
  • How often do you eat out? What's your favorite restaurant?
  • Do you go to the movies often? What kind of movies do you like best?
  • What do you usually do when you have free time?
  • What do you usually do on Saturdays?
  • What do you usually do on your birthday?
  • What's your favorite day of the week, and why?
  • What do you usually do in the evening?
  • What time do you usually go to bed?

Day 1 기본문법

동사 뒤엔 뭐가 오는가

패턴 주어 + 동사 + 목적어

예문 I love him.

  1. 나는 커피를 좋아해. → I ___ coffee.
  2. 그는 그녀를 믿어. → He ___ her.
  3. 우리는 그 영화를 봤어. → We ___ the movie.
  4. 그들은 답을 알아. → They ___ the answer.
  5. 나는 널 이해해. → I ___ you.
  6. 그녀는 개를 키워. → She ___ a dog.
  7. 우리는 그 계획을 좋아해. → We ___ the plan.
  8. 그는 진실을 말했어. → He ___ the truth.
  9. 나는 그 노래를 알아. → I ___ that song.
  10. 그들은 그 집을 샀어. → They ___ the house.
